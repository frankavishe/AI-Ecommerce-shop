Returns and refunds / Kurudisha bidhaa

Returns (English)

You can return any item within 7 days of delivery if it is unused, in its original packaging, and you
have the order number. Shoes and clothes that don't fit can be exchanged for another size or returned
for a refund. Underwear, opened cosmetics and food items cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

To start a return, message us with your order number and the phone number used at checkout. A rider
collects the item in Dar es Salaam at no cost; outside Dar es Salaam you send it by bus and we refund
the bus fee when the return is accepted.

Refunds go back to the same mobile money number or card used to pay, within 3 working days after we
receive and check the item.

Warranty: electronics (phones, chargers, speakers, TVs, blenders) have a 6-month warranty against
factory faults. Keep your order number. Damage from water, drops or wrong voltage is not covered.

Kurudisha bidhaa (Kiswahili)

Unaweza kurudisha bidhaa yoyote ndani ya siku 7 tangu kuletewa kama haijatumika, iko kwenye kifungashio
chake cha awali, na una namba ya oda. Viatu na nguo zisizokutosha zinaweza kubadilishwa saizi au
kurudishwa na kurejeshewa pesa. Nguo za ndani, vipodozi vilivyofunguliwa na vyakula havirudishwi kwa
sababu za usafi.

Kuanza kurudisha, tutumie ujumbe wenye namba ya oda na namba ya simu uliyotumia kulipia. Mpeleka mizigo
atachukua bidhaa Dar es Salaam bila malipo; nje ya Dar es Salaam utaituma kwa basi na tutakurudishia
nauli ya basi baada ya kukubali bidhaa.

Pesa itarudishwa kwenye namba ile ile ya pesa ya simu au kadi uliyotumia kulipa, ndani ya siku 3 za kazi
baada ya kupokea na kukagua bidhaa.

Dhamana: vifaa vya elektroniki (simu, chaja, spika, runinga, blenda) vina dhamana ya miezi 6 dhidi ya
hitilafu za kiwandani. Hifadhi namba ya oda. Uharibifu wa maji, kuanguka au umeme usio sahihi haulipwi.
