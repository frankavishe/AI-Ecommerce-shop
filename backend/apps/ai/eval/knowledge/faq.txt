Frequently asked questions / Maswali ya mara kwa mara

Payment: we accept mobile money (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa / Mixx by Yas, Airtel Money, HaloPesa) and Visa or
Mastercard cards. You pay when you place the order; we do not take cash on delivery.

Opening hours: our store and customer care are open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 18:00, and Sunday
10:00 to 14:00. Online orders are accepted 24 hours a day.

Store location: Kariakoo, Dar es Salaam. You can collect paid orders from the store.

Accounts: you can shop as a guest with just your name and phone number, or create an account to see
your order history and save your address.

Malipo: tunapokea pesa ya simu (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa / Mixx by Yas, Airtel Money, HaloPesa) na kadi za
Visa au Mastercard. Unalipa wakati wa kuweka oda; hatupokei pesa taslimu mzigo ukifika.

Saa za kazi: duka na huduma kwa wateja vinafunguliwa Jumatatu hadi Jumamosi, saa 2 asubuhi hadi saa
12 jioni, na Jumapili saa 4 asubuhi hadi saa 8 mchana. Oda za mtandaoni zinapokelewa saa 24.

Mahali duka lilipo: Kariakoo, Dar es Salaam. Unaweza kuchukua oda iliyolipwa dukani.

Akaunti: unaweza kununua kama mgeni kwa jina na namba ya simu tu, au kufungua akaunti kuona historia
ya oda zako na kuhifadhi anwani yako.
