Delivery / Usafirishaji

Delivery (English)

We deliver to every region of Tanzania. The delivery fee depends on the region and is shown in the
order summary before you pay.

Dar es Salaam: delivery by our riders within 24 hours of payment (same day for orders paid before
12:00). Arusha, Dodoma, Mwanza, Mbeya and Morogoro: delivery by bus or courier in 2 to 3 days. Other
regions: 3 to 5 days.

You get an SMS when your order leaves our store, and the rider calls you before arriving. Please keep
your phone on. If nobody answers after two attempts, the order returns to the store and we contact you
to arrange a new delivery.

Usafirishaji (Kiswahili)

Tunasafirisha mizigo kwenda mikoa yote ya Tanzania. Gharama ya usafirishaji inategemea mkoa na
inaonyeshwa kwenye muhtasari wa oda kabla ya kulipa.

Dar es Salaam: mzigo unaletwa na wapeleka mizigo wetu ndani ya saa 24 baada ya malipo (siku hiyo hiyo
kwa oda zilizolipwa kabla ya saa 6 mchana). Arusha, Dodoma, Mwanza, Mbeya na Morogoro: kwa basi au
kampuni ya usafirishaji ndani ya siku 2 hadi 3. Mikoa mingine: siku 3 hadi 5.

Utapokea SMS mzigo wako ukitoka dukani, na mpeleka mizigo atakupigia simu kabla ya kufika. Tafadhali
acha simu yako ikiwa wazi. Usipopokea simu mara mbili, mzigo unarudi dukani na tutawasiliana nawe
kupanga upya.
